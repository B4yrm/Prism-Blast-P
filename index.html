This Privacy Policy explains what information Prism Blast ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is used. Prism Blast is a casual block-puzzle game developed by [YOUR NAME / STUDIO NAME].

1. Information We Collect

Prism Blast is designed to collect as little personal information as possible:

  • In-game profile: a nickname and flag emoji you choose yourself, and a randomly generated player ID (e.g. #A1B2C3). No real name, email, or phone number is required or requested.
  • Gameplay data: scores, coins, achievements, and settings, stored locally on your device so your progress is remembered.
  • Optional multiplayer data: if you use Duels, Tournaments, or the World Leaderboard, your chosen nickname, flag, and score are shared with other players of the app so matches and rankings can work.

2. Advertising

Prism Blast may show ads served by Google AdMob to support free play. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can review your ad personalization settings in your device's system settings.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect your real name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or location.

4. Data Storage

Most of your progress (coins, best scores, achievements, preferences) is stored locally on your own device. Multiplayer features (leaderboards, duels, tournaments, gifts) store your chosen nickname, flag, and scores on Google Firebase, a cloud database service, so these features can work across devices. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Prism Blast is a general-audience casual game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous nickname/flag/ID described above.

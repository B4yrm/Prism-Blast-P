Real simulation, no drawing, paired runs: same start, same seeds; the register is collected every 200 steps so both runs sell the same units at the same moments (checked: "flows identical"). Notes
- The realised factor of a run differs from the expected one because the catches are random draws (multinomial) from the weights, and the shelf holds what happened to arrive.
- The game rounds every sale to whole money (Sim.serve). At the start a fresh fish costs 4, so for example one sardine sells for round(4 × 0.764) = 3 (×0.75). The revenue ratio therefore differs from the realised factor by this rounding; the gap shrinks when prices are higher (upgrades).
- Products from the factory keep their original price in T1.4 (species do not change product prices), so scenarios with a factory move less.
- Harbor tiers 2 and 3 use the placeholder catch weights of T1.3; their higher factors are an economic effect of eco (more valuable species), not part of the start-mix acceptance.
